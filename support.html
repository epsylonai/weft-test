Weft — Support

Weft — Solitaire Reimagined is a calm card game: chain cards one rank up or down, bank your hand, clear the board.

Questions, problems or ideas: famed_dower8z@icloud.com. Please mention your device model and the version shown on the end-of-board panel.

Tip: the game sounds best with headphones. Music volume, track and animation level are in Settings (the gear on the end panel, or press and hold the header).
