Weft — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-16

On your device

Weft saves your game, settings and score history on your own device so you can continue playing and see your records. Deleting the app or clearing the browser data removes them.

Shared high scores

For the shared high-score lists, Weft sends your nickname, the result date, score, level reached, longest chain, game seed, rules version and the moves you played, so the server can replay the game and verify the result. It also sends a random device identifier, created on your device, to recognise your own results and to limit repeated submissions. Results saved earlier on your device are uploaded when the service is available. If the service is offline, your local records stay available.

Other players can see your nickname and your verified high-score results. The public lists never show your device identifier or your played moves. You can change your nickname in Settings. No e-mail address and no player account are required.

Where the data is kept

The high-score database is run by Supabase and is located in Ireland, in the European Union. Like any website, the services that deliver the game (GitHub Pages) and the high scores (Supabase) may briefly log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation.

What Weft does not do

No advertising, no analytics and no tracking in this version. If a future version adds advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Your choices

To have your nickname and results removed from the shared lists, or for any privacy question, write to famed_dower8z@icloud.com.
